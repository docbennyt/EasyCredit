# Getting Started with EasyCredit

## What is EasyCredit?

EasyCredit helps you track customer credit and change for your small business—without paperwork, notebooks, or WhatsApp messages.

**Perfect for:**
- Small traders and vendors
- Family businesses
- Shop owners
- Informal sellers
- Agents and resellers
- Anyone managing customer credit

## Core Concept

EasyCredit uses a simple **two-book system**:

### 📕 Credit Book
Track **money customers owe you**
- Customer took goods on credit
- Customer made partial payment
- Customer still owes balance

### 📗 Change Book
Track **money you owe customers**
- You're holding customer's change
- Customer overpaid
- Change waiting to be returned

## Creating Your Account

### Step 1: Access EasyCredit

Visit the EasyCredit web app on your phone or computer.

**Recommended:** Use your mobile phone for the best experience.

### Step 2: Sign Up or Log In

**New Users:**
1. Click "Sign Up"
2. Enter your email address
3. Create a secure password
4. Verify your email (check inbox/spam)
5. Complete your first-time setup

**Returning Users:**
1. Click "Log In"
2. Enter your email and password
3. You're back in!

### Step 3: First-Time Setup (New Users Only)

After creating your account, you'll go through a quick setup:

1. **Create Your First Venture**
   - Enter the name of your business (e.g., "Fresh Fish", "Floor Polish")
   - This keeps each business separate

2. **Add Your First Customer**
   - Enter customer name (e.g., "Mai T")
   - Optionally add phone number
   - Phone helps with follow-ups later

3. **Ready to Track**
   - Choose your first action:
   - **Record Credit** - Customer owes you
   - **Keep Change** - You owe customer

**That's it!** Your account is ready.

## Understanding Your Balance

Every customer has **one balance** that can be:

**Positive (+)** - Customer owes you money
- Example: +$10 means customer owes you $10

**Negative (-)** - You owe customer money (change)
- Example: -$5 means you owe customer $5 change

**Zero (0)** - All settled
- Example: $0 means no money owed either way

## Quick Actions

From the dashboard, you can:

- **Add Record** - Record credit or change
- **View Customers** - See who owes what
- **Check Collections** - View overdue items
- **Review Change Book** - See change you owe
- **View Insights** - Understand your cashflow
