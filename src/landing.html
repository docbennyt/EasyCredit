FAQ

Simple answers before you start.

What is EasyCredit?

EasyCredit is a mobile-first app for tracking customer credit, customer change, follow-ups, and venture cashflow.

Is this a loan app?

No. EasyCredit is not a lending app. It helps business owners manage their own customer records.

Can I manage more than one business?

Yes. Each venture has its own customers, Credit Book, Change Book, dashboard, and insights.

Does it work offline?

EasyCredit is designed as an offline-friendly PWA. Some cloud sync features require internet.

How do I get started?

Use Login / Sign Up first. New users go to onboarding after authentication. Existing users return to their dashboard.
